# AnatoMate Asset Manifest

The KIFARO application is wired to the following asset tree:

- `public/assets/anatomate/year1/orientation/`
- `public/assets/anatomate/year1/foundations/`
- `public/assets/anatomate/year1/practical/`
- `public/assets/anatomate/year2/cns/`
- `public/assets/anatomate/year3/abdomen/`
- `public/assets/anatomate/year3/head-neck/`

## Prepared assets

32 PowerPoint lectures have matching PDF exports (64 binary files total).

### Year 1
- Orientation: Welcome to AnatoMate; How to Study Anatomy
- Foundations: Introduction to Anatomy; Introduction to Skeleton; Joints & Arthrology; Muscles & Myology; Fascia, Spaces & Compartments; Blood Vessels; Lymphatic System; Introduction to Nervous System; Skin & Integumentary System; General Anatomy Integration
- Practical: Scapula Practical

### Year 2 — CNS
- Lecture 30 Development of CNS
- Lecture 31 Meninges
- Lecture 32 Ventricular System & CSF
- Lecture 33 Spinal Cord: External Anatomy
- Lecture 34 Spinal Cord: Internal Structure

Lectures 35–38 are present in the curriculum UI but do not yet have compiled PPT/PDF files in the retrieved asset library.
